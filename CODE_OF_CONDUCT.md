# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free environment for everyone — whatever
your age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Scope

This applies to every Cordango community space:

- **GitHub** — repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and comments
- **Events** — meetups, conferences and online gatherings we host
- **Public representation** — acting as a representative of Cordango anywhere, including social
  media and conferences

## Expected behaviour

- **Be respectful.** Treat people professionally, listen, and be mindful of your words.
- **Be inclusive.** Use inclusive language and leave room for others to contribute.
- **Be collaborative.** Help each other and share what you know.
- **Be accountable.** Own your mistakes and take responsibility for your impact.
- **Be empathetic.** Try to understand perspectives that are not yours.
- **Give and take feedback gracefully.** A review is about the code, and a disagreement about
  design is not a disagreement about a person.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Including but not limited to:

- Harassment, discrimination, or offensive comments about personal characteristics
- Personal attacks, trolling, insults, or deliberate intimidation
- Unwelcome sexual attention, advances, or imagery
- Publishing private information — a physical or email address — without explicit consent
- Sustained disruption of a discussion
- Encouraging or advocating any of the above
- Anything else that would be plainly inappropriate in a professional setting

## Reporting

Email **[hello@cordango.com](mailto:hello@cordango.com)** with the subject line `CONDUCT`.

For a security vulnerability, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Reports are confidential. We will not share your identity with the person you are reporting, or with
anyone else, without your consent. If your report concerns a maintainer, it will be handled by
someone who is not that person.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take fair corrective action in
response to anything they judge to be inappropriate — including editing or removing comments,
commits, code, issues and other contributions, and temporarily or permanently banning anyone whose
behaviour warrants it.

We will explain the reason for a moderation decision to the person affected.
